# **THE CORE CONCEPT**

## **The Music Parallel**

In AI music generation, we learned:

* "Max Martin \+ Maynard Keenan" creates specific fusion  
* Producer style \+ artist voice \= predictable output  
* Negative modifiers (-grunge) remove unwanted elements

**The same principle applies to photography:**

* Photographer style \+ subject \= predictable aesthetic  
* Lighting philosophy \+ composition approach \= signature look  
* Negative modifiers (-snapshot, \-amateur) remove unwanted elements

---

## **Two Applications**

### **1\. Session Recreation**

Generate NEW images that *could have been* from a famous photographer's session:

* Same style, new subject  
* Same aesthetic, new context  
* The shoot that never happened but looks like it did

### **2\. Style Fusion**

Combine photographer aesthetics the way we combine music producers:

* "Leibovitz lighting \+ Avedon minimalism"  
* "Newton's power \+ Lindbergh's naturalism"  
* Create looks that reference without copying

# **SECTION 3: NEGATIVE MODIFIERS**

## **Essential Photography Negatives**

Just like "-grunge" and "-mariachi" in music, photography needs negative modifiers:

### **Quality Negatives**

\-snapshot (removes casual/amateur feel)  
\-amateur (removes technical imperfection)  
\-stock-photo (removes generic commercial feel)  
\-overprocessed (removes HDR/Instagram filter look)  
\-AI-generated-look (removes the "too perfect" AI tell)

### **Lighting Negatives**

\-flat-lighting (removes boring even light)  
\-overexposed (removes blown highlights)  
\-underexposed (removes muddy shadows)  
\-harsh-flash (removes amateur flash look)  
\-ring-light (removes influencer aesthetic)

### **Composition Negatives**

\-centered (if you want dynamic composition)  
\-cluttered (removes busy backgrounds)  
\-cropped-awkwardly (removes bad framing)  
\-full-body-stiff (removes passport photo feel)

### **Mood Negatives**

\-casual (removes informal feel)  
\-candid (if you want posed)  
\-posed (if you want candid)  
\-commercial (removes advertising feel)  
\-editorial (if you want fine art)

### 

### **Style-Specific Negatives**

\-glamour (for raw/authentic looks)  
\-natural (for dramatic/artificial looks)  
\-vintage (for modern looks)  
\-modern (for period looks)  
\-fashion (for documentary/portrait)  
\-portrait (for fashion/editorial)

---

STUDIO-PORTRAIT:

# **SECTION 7: PRACTICAL WORKFLOWS**

## **Workflow 1: Recreating a Famous Session**

**Goal:** Generate images that look like they came from a specific photographer's session

**Steps:**

1. Choose photographer (e.g., Leibovitz)  
2. Identify their signature elements (dramatic lighting, environmental, emotional)  
3. Choose your subject (character or concept)  
4. Build prompt using PHOTOGRAPHER-STYLE template  
5. Add negative modifiers to prevent drift  
6. Generate and iterate

**Example:**

Step 1: Leibovitz  
Step 2: Dramatic chiaroscuro, environmental storytelling, emotional depth  
Step 3: Vela Corazon  
Step 4: "Annie Leibovitz portrait session | dramatic lighting rich shadows |   
        Vela Corazon in cathedral setting religious adornments |  
        emotional depth editorial quality | environmental storytelling |"  
Step 5: "-snapshot \-amateur \-flat-lighting \-overexposed"  
Step 6: Generate, assess, refine

## **Workflow 2: The Impossible Session**

**Goal:** Create a shoot between a photographer and subject that never happened

**Steps:**

1. Choose photographer whose style interests you  
2. Choose subject that never worked with them  
3. Identify the interesting tension/synergy  
4. Build prompt emphasizing the collision  
5. Let the "what if" guide creative choices

**Example:**

"What if Helmut Newton shot Maren Sunday?"

Tension: Newton's dangerous sexuality vs. Maren's wholesome faith  
Synergy: Both deal with powerful women  
Result: Powerful but pure, dangerous but good

Prompt:  
Helmut Newton editorial | high contrast black and white |  
Maren Sunday powerful pose country glamour |  
dangerous elegance but wholesome | cross necklace visible |  
strong woman of faith | fashion noir meets heartland |  
\-soft \-innocent \-weak \-casual

## **Workflow 3: Style Evolution Series**

**Goal:** Show same subject through multiple photographer lenses

**Steps:**

1. Choose one subject/character  
2. Select 4-6 contrasting photographers  
3. Generate same subject through each style  
4. Create series showing range of interpretation

**Example Series: Seren Seiko**

1\. Avedon — stark minimal precision  
2\. Newton — powerful dangerous precision  
3\. Penn — sculptural elegant precision  
4\. Lindbergh — raw natural precision  
5\. LaChapelle — surreal maximalist precision  
6\. Walker — fantastical whimsical precision

Each image shows Seren through a different lens while maintaining her core identity.
